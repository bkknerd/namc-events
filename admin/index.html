How to edit the site

  1. Pick a section in the list on the left (for example Home page or Registration and prices), then pick the item inside it.
  2. Change the text or numbers. The panel on the right shows roughly how it will look on the site.
  3. Photos: click a photo box, then Choose an image to upload one. Square or portrait photos about 800 pixels wide work best.
  4. Press Publish at the top, then Publish now. Your change is live on the website in about a minute.

Good to know

  • Change a price once and it updates on every page. In text boxes you can type {{tk.eb}} for the Early Bird price, {{tk.std}} for Standard, and {{tk.bo}} for the booth price.
  • Never change a Code on an existing ticket or sponsor level. The site uses it behind the scenes.
  • Made a mistake? Do not worry. Every change is saved with history, so it can be undone.
